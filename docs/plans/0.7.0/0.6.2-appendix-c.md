| WP-14b's known edges (minor, open). **`registry.missed` can be wrong three ways**, which Session B tells apart by each event's trigger and time: (1) too high: a read that sees a change and lands before that change's notification counts a miss (a miss followed by a `notify` refresh within about 1 s); (2) possibly too high around a wake: a notification queued during sleep can land after the first read after the wake (a `history`-triggered miss right after a wake; how often is unverified); (3) too low: any change notification counts, Low Power Mode and thermal ones included, and any later notification hides an earlier miss. With power in the menu bar (cached 10-s polls; no history read, since a read always came within 20 s) a miss shows only when the next fetch is the 5-minute safety net with no notification in between, so the detector is nearly blind there; it is fine-grained only while Power or Raw Data is shown (a fetch every 2 s) or with no polling (the history read each minute), so Session B counts it per hour of such runtime. `followUp`-triggered events are updates that the follow-up re-reads already catch. Also: `apply()` records a fetch by the scope the monitor asked for, so a `.cached` request that the provider turns into a fetch (an empty cache after a read without a battery) isn't recorded; the next safety-net re-read can come early and one baseline is skipped, values unaffected.
